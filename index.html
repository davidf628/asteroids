Rectilinear Motion Calculus Demonstrator

Controls: LEFT Arrow = Accelerate (-x direction), RIGHT Arrow = Accelerate (+x direction). UP/DOWN Arrows = Rotate ship (2D mode). SPACEBAR = Shoot lasers. P = Pause/Unpause simulation.

Calculus Focus: Notice that when velocity and acceleration have the same sign, the ship's speed increases ("accelerating"). When velocity and acceleration have opposite signs, the ship's speed decreases ("decelerating").
